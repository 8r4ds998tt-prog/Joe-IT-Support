That thing you mentioned

You were right about the tracking

You reckoned GoDaddy doesn't properly support Google Ads conversion tracking, and that you might be paying for clicks that never get near your contact form.

Your instinct was sound. GoDaddy's builder genuinely won't let you put tracking code where Google normally needs it. It's a real limitation of the platform — not something you've set up wrong.

There are ways round it using the Google settings already built into your dashboard, and month one is finding out how far we can get.

It might not end up perfect. But even if we can't track every last thing, I can find out whether people who click your ads are reaching your contact page at all — which is really what you were asking.
